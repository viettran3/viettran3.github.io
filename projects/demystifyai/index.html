DemystifyAI

An AI-literacy Chrome extension that teaches people how AI chat tools work, right inside the tools they already use.

Role
Product Engineer Intern
When
Summer 2026
Where
Chicago, IL
01problem02process03final04impact

Problem

AI chat tools went mainstream faster than people learned how they work. Most people meet them cold: they don't know what the tool is good at, where it gets things wrong, or what happens to what they type. DemystifyAI runs AI-literacy workshops in Chicago communities, including with the Chicago Housing Authority, and wanted that learning to keep going after the workshop ended.

To add One or two sentences in Viet's words on the specific gap the team saw (who struggled, with what).

Process

  • Listen first. I analyzed pre- and post-workshop surveys across two AI-literacy sessions to see what people understood, what confused them, and what they wanted next.
  • Co-design with residents. With the Chicago Housing Authority, we folded resident feedback directly into both the workshops and the product.
  • Meet people where they are. Instead of a separate course, the extension brings short, contextual learning moments into the AI chat tools people already use.
  • Design for trust. Accessibility and user privacy were constraints from day one, not polish at the end.
