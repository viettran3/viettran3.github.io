01 About

I was born in Vietnam and grew up in a small farming town in rural Washington, where a lot of what I learned came from building things with whatever was around: Arduino robots, a lab project on pulling microplastics out of water, and an FBLA chapter that led a $68,000+ renovation of our town's Old Town Hall.

Today I'm at UChicago, leaning toward Cognitive Science alongside Economics or Media, Arts, Data & Design. I like the space where people meet technology: why an interface feels obvious or confusing, and how to make powerful tools feel calm.

This summer I worked as a Product Engineer Intern at DemystifyAI and a UI/UX Design Intern at Bright Bots, and I design my own suite of personal-assistant apps that I use every day. In Summer 2027 I'll be a Software Development Engineer Intern at Amazon.
